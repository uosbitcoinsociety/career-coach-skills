Your free AI careers coach

Mock interviews, video-interview practice, CV help and an application tracker. No sign-up, nothing to install. It works with the ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini account you already have, free ones included.

How it works

  1. Pick what you want to practise and fill in the boxes. The more detail you give, the better the coaching.
  2. Press Open in ChatGPT (or Claude / Gemini). Your coaching instructions are copied for you.
  3. If the chat box is empty, paste: Ctrl+V on a laptop, or long-press on a phone. Then press send.

Your details only go to the AI you choose. This site has no server and doesn't save anything you type here.
